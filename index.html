A realistic portrait in nine small steps

Draw the shadows first.
The face follows.

Drop in a reference photo. Portrait Coach breaks it into nine small steps, from a few straight lines to a finished, realistic drawing. Each step shows you what your paper should look like next.

Your recent photos

  1. Lines. Outline the head and the shadow shapes with light, straight lines.
  2. Tones. Fill the shadows, then the darkest darks, then the half-tones.
  3. Finish. Blend smooth, add the details, lift the highlights.

You'll need 2H, B and 6B pencils, a tissue or blending stump, and a kneaded eraser.
